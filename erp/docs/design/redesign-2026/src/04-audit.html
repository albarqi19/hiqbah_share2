05

Audit of the current ERP

Evaluated against the patterns above. Every finding has an ID used in the roadmap, evidence you can re-run, the impact on a person doing the job, and the specific change.

What already works, and must survive the redesign

One navigation registry

lib/nav/registry.ts drives sidebar, contextual tabs, breadcrumbs, active state and the route guard from one tree, with requiresAll/unlessAny placement rules. Keep it; change its labels and depth (§10), not its mechanism.

Domain honesty in the UI copy

Won/Lost are outcomes, not stages; "a sample does not move stock"; fulfilment and payment are separate; counts state that they reflect filters. The content is right. The placement (paragraphs above every list) is what changes.

Separate order statuses with distinct meaning

The --oo-status-* comments distinguish Rejected (order-level) from Blocked (item-level) and keep Cancelled neutral, not red. That thinking becomes the global status registry (§9).

Tabular numerals and logical properties already in use

109 tabular-nums and 270 logical-direction utilities show the right instincts. The redesign makes them defaults instead of per-component choices.

Lead table "next action" column

Each lead row offers the one action its state allows (تحويل إلى عميل, تسجيل متابعة, تحديد موعد متابعة). This is the right model; it should become a row action pattern used across modules.

Unsaved-changes guard and stacked tables for the floor

useUnsavedGuard and the .stack-table rationale (operators at 1024px losing their row) come from real observation. Keep the intent; change the mechanisms (§11).

Evidence: current screens

  1. Dark sidebar and violet active pill are the highest-contrast objects on the page; the work area is grey on grey.
  2. The active sidebar item is إدارة العملاء, a container name. "Leads" exists only as a tab, so the sidebar never names the page you are on.
  3. A 38px provisional banner with margins (about 90px of height), then the title, then three lines of explanatory text before the first control.
  4. Primary button is indigo #4F46E5; active nav is violet #7C3AED. Two brand colours.
  5. "Custom Role" and "HIQBAH" in Latin inside an Arabic shell.
  6. Empty state has no action; the create button is 600px away.
  1. Jan 1, 2099: an English-locale date and what looks like a sentinel value leaking into an Arabic table. lib/utils.ts formatDate() hard-codes "en-US".
  2. Status pills are outlined, 32px tall, with a different icon each; "overdue" and "no next commitment" use the same shape as statuses.
  3. Row actions vary from 96px to 180px wide and wrap (غير مؤهّل — لا تحويل on two lines), so the column edge is ragged.
  4. Rows are about 66px tall (CSS px): roughly 9 leads fit on a 1440×900 screen after the header block. At the proposed 40px rows, 15 fit.
  1. Every card carries four buttons (السابقة، التالية، رابح، خاسر): with 40 open deals that is 160 buttons on one screen.
  2. Stage names are prefixed "01 … 04"; the number adds nothing the order does not already say.
  3. Stage value sits under the title in grey; there is no weighted value and no stall indicator.
  4. Won and Lost occupy half the page as two framed panels, pushing nothing useful.
  5. The mobile version labels the same actions ربح / خسارة instead of رابح / خاسر.
  1. Five header buttons of similar weight; تعليم خاسر is outlined red next to the primary.
  2. The stage stepper lives in its own 170px card with a sub-caption explaining that stages are configurable.
  3. Seven separate cards on one page; properties are pushed to a narrow column at the far end with large gaps.
  4. Activity entries are boxed cards with icons; history is a separate card that repeats stage changes.
  1. The stage bars are scaled by deal count but labelled with value. 124,000 and 50,000 render at the same length because both stages hold 2 deals (sales/reports/page.tsx:98, 223).
  2. Eight KPI cards in two rows with no ordering by importance; three-decimal-place percentages (38.10%).
  3. Lead sources, loss reasons and cycle length are grey pill lists inside cards rather than a sortable table or bar list.
