Self-review: what still felt generic, and what changed

Each screen was rendered at 1440px and inspected after the first build. These are the faults found and the changes made in round two. All of them are visible in the screens above.

Exec chart read as decline.
The daily line was jagged (weekend dips) and, with time running right to left, a rising month read as a fall to anyone used to Latin charts. It also ran past the page gutter.
Cumulative month-to-date against the same days of August, so the gap is the story. Quantitative charts now run left to right, as Saudi banking and market apps do; schedules and process tracks stay right to left. Width fitted to the content column.
The roaster track was a toy.
Squeezed into a third of the page, its labels clipped to “خلط”, and the batches were an hour long on a W15, which roasts 15 kg, not 24.
It is now a full-width band and the signature of the operations page: a W30, roughly 30-minute cycles on a 07:00–13:00 track, a hatched maintenance slot, and the live 10:42 line. Day figures were corrected to match three finished batches (61.2 kg).
The orders table hid under its own drawer.
An overlay drawer covered the payment column, and filter chips wrapped onto two lines.
A split view: the list stays fully visible and drops its secondary columns (quantity, city, owner) while the preview is open. A note says the columns come back on close. Chips and search no longer wrap.
Two primary buttons per row.
“تأكيد الشحن” rendered as a filled button in every ready row, and a class clash greyed its label.
Row actions are all secondary. The page header keeps the only primary action.
Brand noise in the chrome.
A floating Latin “ERP” label, a bell with an ember dot that duplicated the inbox count, and an avatar whose “إ” read as “!”.
The wordmark plus an Arabic descriptor; the bell is gone because the inbox is the notification centre; the avatar uses two initials (إع).
Bidi slips.
Week ranges in the bar chart rendered as “29–22”, “1 × 750 غ” reordered, and the PO number aligned to the wrong edge above its title.
Ranges and quantities are LTR-isolated, and IDs are inline isolates rather than block-level LTR.

Still weak, honestly

The sidebar is conventional.
Seventeen items with icons is the Linear/Attio pattern. It is calm, but not distinctive.
Next step: role-scoped navigation, so a roaster sees about 6 items and the owner sees everything, plus pinned views.
The paired cash bars are generic.
They are honest and readable, but any SaaS product could have drawn them.
Candidate: a cash-gap view that shows days of sales not yet collected per week, which ties back to the 11-day lag in the lede.
Only desktop at 1440px.
Tablet (the floor) and phone (reps, approvals) are not drawn in this direction yet.
The production page and the ops roaster band should be drawn next at 1024px with touch density.
Dark mode is only defined.
Tokens exist for dark, but no screen has been reviewed in it.
Light ships first; dark needs its own review pass.
